# Code of Conduct

Hardless follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in Hardless to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Danish-Ali097/hardless/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code or issues that break this code, and may ban
contributors temporarily or permanently, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
