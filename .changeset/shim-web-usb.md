WebUSB (PRD section 8.2): `navigator.usb` with `requestDevice()` (filters, exclusion filters, picker, `picker-cancel`), `getDevices()`, `connect` / `disconnect` events, and `USBDevice` for emulated USB printers: printer-class descriptors, enforced `open` / `claimInterface` order, `transferOut`, `transferIn`, the `GET_DEVICE_ID` and `GET_PORT_STATUS` control requests, and the `stall` and `interface-busy` faults.
