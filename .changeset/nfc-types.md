NFC types: `NdefRecord`, `NfcReading`, `NfcEvent` and `NfcRejection`; the bridge `nfc-write`, `nfc-make-read-only`, `nfc-reading` and `nfc-error` messages; the `not_allowed` error code; `nfc` event log records. `ControlCommand` gains `nfc-write` and `nfc-make-read-only`, and a tap's `records` are now typed and optional.
